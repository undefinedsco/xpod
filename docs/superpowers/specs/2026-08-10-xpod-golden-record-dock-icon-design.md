# Xpod Golden Record Dock Icon Design

## Goal

Create a recognizable macOS Dock icon for Xpod that evokes a golden record as a durable vessel for personal data, memory, and knowledge. It must read as Xpod rather than as a music player.

## Composition

- Use the standard macOS squircle silhouette with safe optical padding.
- Retain Xpod's deep purple as the background, with restrained depth rather than a flat fill.
- Place one abstract golden disc at the visual center.
- Construct the disc from three or four incomplete concentric tracks. The tracks suggest recorded data and accumulated memory without imitating a vinyl record.
- Offset the center aperture slightly to introduce quiet rotational movement.
- Cut a small geometric interruption into the lower-right track structure. Its negative space should imply an `X` without becoming a literal letterform.

## Material and Light

- Use matte, warm gold with subtle tonal variation.
- Avoid chrome, glitter, lens flares, neon glow, and high-contrast photorealism.
- Use a soft upper-left light source and restrained inner shadow so the icon remains native beside current macOS icons.
- Keep the purple background darker at the perimeter and slightly warmer behind the disc.

## Small-size Requirements

- At 16–32 px, the icon must still read as a gold disc on purple.
- Fine grooves may merge, but the outer disc, aperture, and lower-right interruption must remain distinct.
- No text, micro-symbols, or thin strokes that disappear in the Dock.

## Deliverables

- One 1024×1024 master PNG.
- A complete macOS iconset and `icon.icns` generated from that master.
- Updated desktop application package and DMG.
- Visual verification at 16, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512, and 1024 px, plus a Dock-context preview.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Immediately distinguishable from the current plain purple placeholder.
- Suggests a golden record and stored memory without looking like a media-player app.
- Maintains clear silhouette and contrast at Dock sizes.
- Matches Xpod's purple visual language and macOS icon conventions.
